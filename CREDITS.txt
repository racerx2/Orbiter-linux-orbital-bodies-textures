Planet and moon tiles (TileFormat 2) for the Linux port of Orbiter
https://github.com/racerx2/orbiter-linux

Unzip a package into the Orbiter folder; it adds Textures/<Body>/Surf or Cloud.
md5.txt has the checksums. Earth, Moon, Mars and the minor bodies are the
Orbiter packages in urls.txt.

Ganymede: Askaniy Anpilogov, USGS, Björn Jónsson and NASA/JPL-Caltech/SwRI/MSSS/Brian Swift, Ganymede map (2019)
Callisto: Askaniy Anpilogov and John van Vliet, Callisto map (2019)
Saturn: Askaniy Anpilogov and Björn Jónsson, Saturn map
  Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0; the tiles made from these three maps are under the same licence
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ganymede_map_by_Askaniy.png
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Callisto_map_by_Askaniy.png
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Saturn_map_by_Askaniy.png

Io: USGS Astrogeology Science Center, Io Galileo SSI/Voyager Color Merged Global Mosaic 1 km
Europa: USGS Astrogeology Science Center, Europa Voyager and Galileo SSI Global Mosaic 500 m;
  colour fitted on the Galileo colour image PIA19048 (NASA/JPL-Caltech/SETI Institute)
  https://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/catalog/PIA19048

Mercury: LobedHomunculus, Mercury texture map from the USGS MESSENGER MDIS global colour mosaic,
  Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mercury_Texture_Map.png

Uranus: Solar System Scope, CC BY 4.0
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Solarsystemscope_texture_2k_uranus.jpg

Triton: Paul Schenk, Lunar and Planetary Institute, Voyager 2 global map (NASA/JPL-Caltech/LPI),
  on the earlier Orbiter map where Voyager saw nothing
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Triton_map_no_grid.jpg

Pluto and Charon: New Horizons LORRI/MVIC global mosaics, 300 m/px (NASA/Johns Hopkins University
  Applied Physics Laboratory/Southwest Research Institute/Lunar and Planetary Institute);
  Schenk et al., Icarus 314 and 315 (2018); colour from the New Horizons MVIC global colour
  mosaics (PDS Small Bodies Node, doi:10.26007/mc7j-ef52)
  https://planetarymaps.usgs.gov/mosaic/

Venus and its clouds, Titan and its clouds, Hyperion, Miranda, Ariel, Umbriel, Titania, Oberon,
Neptune, Triton's clouds, Proteus, Nereid: made from the textures that ship with Orbiter
(see Orbiter's credits).
